Agents

Agents are Buddy doing things for you: tasks, routines and "do it for me". Choose how much they may do without asking, and where they may act at all.

Trusted apps

Never act in

Agents won't click, type or move windows in these apps at all, even if you'd approve. Password managers are listed to start with.

Folders Buddy reads without asking

Buddy can look in these folders and read the files in them without asking you first. Anywhere else, it asks each time. Making or changing a file always asks. Its own Documents › Buddy folder is always on the list.

Let Buddy run commands

For bigger jobs: renaming or sorting many files, converting, building things. Buddy asks before every command and says what it will do, but a command can change or delete things Buddy can't put back, and Buddy can't fully fence in what a command reaches. Leave it off unless you need it. Off, Buddy still works in your apps and makes and edits files, which it can undo.

Routines

Tasks Buddy starts on a schedule, each in its own window tucked into the island. They follow your Agents rules, so anything that needs your OK waits for you.

Routines run while Buddy is running. One missed by less than six hours (the PC was asleep) runs when Buddy next can; older ones are skipped.

Lessons

When Buddy walks you through something all the way, it offers to keep the steps as a lesson. Play one to be shown again, in the app it's for: Buddy points at each step and follows you, without asking the AI, so it costs nothing. A lesson only points: it never clicks or types. Share one as a file, and import lessons people share with you.

Activity

What Buddy did on your PC this past week: files it made or changed, documents, commands it ran, pages it read, and what it clicked, pressed or typed in your apps. A file can be put back with Undo: one Buddy made goes to the Recycle Bin, one it changed gets its old version back. What Buddy did in an app is undone in that app.

About & updates

Desktop Buddy is open source. You bring your own Anthropic API key; everything else runs on this PC.

Check for updates automatically

Buddy looks at its GitHub releases now and then and downloads a new version in the background. It installs when you restart Buddy.

Report a problem

Buddy puts together its version, your settings (without keys, addresses or routines) and its recent timing log, with anything that looks like a key or a file in your folder taken out. You see every word first, and nothing is sent unless you choose to.

This is everything that would be sent. Opening an issue takes you to GitHub, where you add what happened, and can change or remove anything, before you submit it.

AI & privacy

Buddy thinks with the AI you choose: a provider on your own API key, or a model running on this PC. It can also work inside the AI app you already use (Claude, Codex, Gemini, Copilot), on your own plan. Questions, and what's on screen when you ask, go to the AI you choose; with a model on this PC, they stay here. Besides that, Buddy only downloads its voice models once and checks GitHub for updates.

Window screenshots

When you ask, Buddy includes a picture of that window to understand charts and layouts. With a cloud AI, the picture goes to your provider. Off, Buddy reads window text instead. Mark and Explain still use the area you choose.
